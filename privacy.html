Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

COLD INK Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that uploads the COLD INK creator's own videos to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

Data the app uses

  • Access tokens issued by Google, TikTok and Meta after the account owner signs in.
  • Basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The owner's own videos and captions, which the app uploads.

How the data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never published or sent anywhere except to the platform that issued them. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not read viewers' data.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the security settings of Google, TikTok or Meta, and delete the local token files. Nothing else is kept.
